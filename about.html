The Artisanal Narrative

THE SOUL OF
Mander

A village rooted in craft, culture, and community in the heart of Jharkhand. Mandar is home to artisan families who continue to practice the centuries-old tradition of Dhokra metal casting.

Our Legacy

BORN FROM THE EARTH,
Refined by Generations

Mander didn't begin in a boardroom; it began in a small workshop nestled in the valley. For over a century, our family has curated the relationship between human intent and natural raw materials.

We believe that every object carries the memory of its creation. The slight imperfection in a hand-thrown bowl, the deep grain of walnut—these aren't flaws, they are the fingerprints of the craft.

Workshop Life

THE HUMAN
Pulse

Life at the Mander studio follows the sun, not the clock. We begin with a shared breakfast—a time to discuss the day's projects and the materials we'll be handling.

There is a communal silence here, broken only by the rhythmic strike of a chisel or the soft whir of a wheel. It is an environment of intense focus and mutual respect, where the newest apprentice sits beside the master of forty years.

"We don't just build objects; we build a culture of presence."
