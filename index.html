Rooted in Mander

Located around 30 km west of Ranchi, Mandar is a village and community development block in Jharkhand known for its rich craft traditions. Surrounded by local artisan communities, the region continues to preserve the centuries-old practice of Dhokra metal casting through generations of skilled makers.
