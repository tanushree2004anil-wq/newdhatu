The Earth and Wheel: A Meditative Pottery Session

location_on Tuscany, Italy schedule 4 Hours group Up to 4 People

Join master craftsman Elio Moretti in his private hillside atelier for an immersive journey into the soul of Tuscan clay. This is more than a lesson; it is an editorial exploration of texture, rhythm, and the tactile language of the earth.

You will begin with a walk through the local olive groves to understand the mineral composition of the soil before returning to the studio. Here, time slows down as you learn the foundational techniques of centering, pulling, and trimming, all while enjoying local vintages and artisanal snacks.
